# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in nsevt respectful and free from harassment
for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, document technical claims, and focus
criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
personal attacks, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to salomon.eli@colpos.mx. The maintainer may
edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
